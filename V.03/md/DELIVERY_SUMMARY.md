| Medium Text | #64748B | Secondary text |
| Success | #16A34A | Green states |
| Warning | #EAB308 | Yellow states |
| Error | #DC2626 | Red states |

### Typography
- **Font**: System fonts (-apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto)
- **Base**: 16px (1rem)
- **Scale**: H1 (32px) → Micro (12px)
- **Weights**: 400, 500, 600, 700

### Spacing
- **Base**: 8px (0.5rem = 8px)
- **Grid**: Multiples of 8px
- **Padding**: 0.5rem → 2rem
- **Gap**: 0.5rem → 2rem

### Components
- **Radius**: 12px (cards), 8px (buttons/inputs), 6px (badges)
- **Shadow**: Soft drop (0 1px 3px), Lift (0 4px 12px)
- **Transitions**: 0.2s - 0.3s ease
